AI Product Classification & UGC Review Generation

Three Claude-powered agents on Airtable: 92% accurate product taxonomy automation, AI-generated social proof from delivery photos, and order visualizations with material swatches

Role

Systems Architect & AI Integration Lead

Company

FullFlat LLC

Location

United Arab Emirates

Year

2026

Core Tech

Airtable Claude Sonnet Telegram API AI Image Generation

The Challenge

FullFlat sources furniture from multiple contract manufacturers, receiving updated product feeds every 12 hours. Manual classification into the company's 7-level taxonomy (class, category, subcategory, model, series, options, metadata) was the bottleneck—every product needed human review before publication. Separately, the storefront lacked social proof: only customer purchases generated reviews, and hundreds of delivery photos from the logistics partner sat untapped. Manually moderating and matching UGS photos to products was time-consuming and error-prone. On top of that, customers ordering multiple pieces had no way to see how their selections would look together—color and material combinations across a sofa, bed, side tables, and accent pieces were impossible to preview before delivery, creating uncertainty and occasional post-purchase dissatisfaction.

Overview

Three AI agents built natively in Airtable using Claude Sonnet. The first automatically classifies products from 12-hourly supplier feeds into a company-standard 7-level taxonomy at 92% accuracy, with real-time Telegram QA alerts. The second transforms delivery photos (UGC) into social proof by analyzing them, pulling real CSAT reviews when available, generating synthetic reviews when not, creating random avatars, and matching photos to products in the catalog. The third generates furniture layout visualizations with material swatches—for individual catalog products and for full customer orders—pulling from a material library linked to each product variation, and sending the result straight to the client.

Solution

Built three AI agents as Airtable automations, all powered by Claude Sonnet. The first runs on new supplier products and auto-classifies them into company standards; the second transforms delivery photos into social proof without manual review; the third generates furniture visualizations on demand, either for a single catalog item or an entire client order.

Agent 1: Product Classifier

Triggered whenever a new product appears in the supplier feed table, the agent receives the product data (name, description, price, attributes) and sends it to Claude Sonnet via a pre-configured Airtable prompt. The prompt is hardcoded with FullFlat's taxonomy rules and formatting standards. Claude classifies the product and returns: product class, category, subcategory, model, series, options, SEO title, and SEO description—all pre-formatted to company spec and ready for the PIM. On success, the agent sends a real-time Telegram notification with the product name and a direct link to the Airtable record for instant QA review.

The agent achieves 92% accuracy on taxonomy placement, meaning most products are classified correctly on first pass. The remaining 8% catches edge cases or ambiguous products that need human review—a huge improvement over having to manually check every single new product.

Agent 2: Review Generator

Triggered when delivery photos are uploaded to the Airtable by the logistics partner's integration, the agent analyzes each photo using Claude's vision capabilities. It checks whether CSAT data exists for that order. If yes, it pulls the customer review and rating from the CSAT record. If no CSAT exists, it asks Claude to generate a plausible review based on the product and photo context—ensuring the storefront never lacks social proof even when real feedback isn't available.

Next, the agent generates a random avatar image via Airtable's built-in image generation field. This ensures each review appears as a distinct person without needing to source or buy stock photos. Finally, it uses image analysis to match the photo to a product in the catalog—determining which furniture piece or room setting is shown—and links the review to that product. The complete review (text, rating, avatar, matched product) is then published to the storefront.

Agent 3: Order & Product Visualizer

This agent generates furniture layout visualizations pulling swatches from a material library linked to each product variation—shown above as an example: sofa, bed, side tables, lamp, and nightstands laid out together with their available finish options. It works two ways: on a single catalog product, to show a customer the material and color options for that item, or on a full customer order, combining every piece the customer bought into one coherent visualization with their exact selected finishes.

When triggered on an order, the agent pulls each ordered item's variation record, retrieves the linked materials from the shared material library, and composes a layout image showing the full order together—rather than customers imagining how five separately-photographed pieces might look side by side in their chosen colors. The finished visualization is sent directly to the client via email or WhatsApp, giving them a clear preview of their order before it ships.

Integration & Workflow

All three agents run as Airtable automations, triggered by record changes in their respective tables. No external infrastructure, no servers to manage—just Airtable's native automation coupled with Claude Sonnet API calls. The product classifier sends alerts to a dedicated Telegram channel that the team monitors during business hours. The review generator publishes directly to the storefront via a pre-configured webhook, making reviews live within minutes of delivery confirmation. The visualizer sends its output straight to the client, closing the loop between order confirmation and delivery with a clear visual preview.
