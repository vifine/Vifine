FullFlat Operations

Role

Systems Architect & COO

Client / Location

FullFlat LLC / UAE

Core Tech

Airtable · n8n · Shopify · Airpower

Year

2025 — 2026

Overview

The central hub of the FullFlat platform. Manages the full order lifecycle — from customer inquiry to delivery confirmation and post-delivery claims. All teams work within a single Airtable interface with role-specific views: sales managers handle orders and quoting, the fulfillment team manages procurement and supplier coordination, delivery supervisors confirm shipments from mobile, and management monitors performance through built-in reports.

The system covers five core processes: Order Intake & Sales Pipeline, Fulfillment & Procurement, Delivery & On-site Supervision, Claims Management, and Reporting & Plan vs. Fact.

Order Intake & Sales Pipeline

Orders enter the system from two channels: the Shopify storefront and WhatsApp consultations. Shopify orders sync automatically to FullFlat Operations via Airpower. WhatsApp inquiries — typical for high-ticket and customized furniture orders — are handled by sales managers directly in the system. The manager selects products, auto-calculates margin and cost, generates a proforma invoice with a payment link, and creates a visual moodboard from PIM product images to send to the client for approval.

Fulfillment & Delivery

Once payment is confirmed, the order moves to fulfillment. The system splits the order into Fulfillment Orders — one per supplier. A single customer order can generate multiple fulfillment orders when items come from different suppliers, each with its own cost, status and timeline. The fulfillment team tracks all orders through the Fulfillment Calendar, which provides a visual overview of upcoming and in-progress fulfillment tasks across the team.

When goods are ready, the system consolidates items from multiple suppliers into a delivery. An on-site supervisor receives a Delivery Task with a full list of items. During delivery and furniture assembly, the supervisor marks each item as delivered or not delivered with a reason — directly from the Airtable mobile app. A photo report is uploaded on-site. After delivery is complete, the system collects a customer satisfaction rating (CSAT).

Reviews Pipeline

Delivery photo reports feed directly into FullFlat Reviews — a separate system that automates customer review generation. After delivery, the CSAT manager collects customer feedback and permission to publish. The review is composed from the delivery photos (taken by the supervisor on-site) and the customer's feedback — solving the problem of customers being reluctant to photograph and review on their own.

Reviews are synced to the Shopify storefront with customer avatar, rating, review text and real delivery photos.

How operational design transformed the metrics that matter.

  • End-to-end order lifecycle managed in a single system — eliminating spreadsheets and disconnected tools
  • Real-time margin visibility at order level enables data-driven decisions on discounts, upsells and package composition
  • Moodboard and proforma invoice generation within the system shortens the sales cycle for high-ticket WhatsApp orders
  • Multi-supplier fulfillment with automatic order splitting gives the team flexibility to optimize cost and delivery time per item
  • On-site delivery confirmation from mobile with photo reports ensures accountability and feeds directly into CSAT tracking
  • Delivery photo reports converted into published customer reviews — solving the UGC challenge without relying on customers to photograph and post themselves
  • Full operational transparency across sales, fulfillment and delivery — management sees pipeline, margins, supplier performance and customer satisfaction in one place
