Product Information Management System

Single source of truth for 10,000+ SKUs — automated supplier feeds, AI-powered content, and multi-channel Shopify publishing

Role

Architect

Company

FullFlat LLC

Location

UAE

Year

2025

Core Tech

Airtable Shopify API n8n Photoroom Decor8

Overview

Single source of truth for all product data across the business. Manages the full product lifecycle — from initial creation to Shopify publication. The system serves three key roles: content manager (product data and visuals), procurement (supplier pricing), and SEO specialist. The PIM system is connected with AI content generation and image processing services including Photoroom and Decor8, which are used to generate and enhance product images and marketing content before publishing.

The Challenge

Product data lived across disconnected sources — supplier pricing sheets, catalog spreadsheets, and inconsistent product descriptions. There was no single source of truth for SKUs, no structured SEO workflow, and publishing to Shopify required manual, error-prone data entry across 10,000+ products.

Solution

The system manages the full product lifecycle across two entry paths, a structured enrichment workflow, and multi-channel publishing to Shopify and beyond.

Product Entry & Supplier Feeds

Products enter PIM through two paths. Own furniture is created directly in the system — product, variants, materials, and 3D-rendered visuals. Accessories are imported from supplier feeds with automated sync of catalog data, pricing, stock levels, and images. The feed interface tracks sync status and update logs for every product, ensuring data consistency between supplier systems and PIM.

Enrichment, SEO & Publishing

Each product goes through a structured enrichment process: taxonomy mapping, product attributes and metafields, SEO titles and descriptions. The SEO specialist works in a dedicated view across 1,000+ products, tracking readiness status for each item.

Ready products are published to Shopify with full control over publishing channels — Online Store, Meta, Google & YouTube, Pinterest. Each product carries its taxonomy-driven collections, tags, metafields, and media. Sync status is tracked per product with two methods: Airpower for bulk updates and Shopify API for individual products with variants and bundles.
