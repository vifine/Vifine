E-commerce Electronics Store

Role

Systems Architect & Developer

Client / Location

Nordic Electronics GmbH / Germany

Core Tech

Shopify, Stripe, HubSpot CRM, n8n

Year

2025

Project Overview

Centralized Data Model

By centralizing product data, the team eliminated manual spreadsheet updates and reduced the time it took to launch new SKUs across markets. This foundation also enabled more accurate inventory reporting and faster troubleshooting when discrepancies arose.

Unified Product Information

A single source of truth for product data, pricing, and localized VAT rules — managed in one place and synced automatically across the storefront and fulfillment systems.

Automated Fulfillment Routing

Orders are routed instantly to the nearest fulfillment hub based on customer location, reducing transit times and improving delivery reliability across the region.

Real-time Customer Profiles

Every transaction creates a deep customer profile in HubSpot CRM, enabling targeted support, retention campaigns, and a more personalized shopping experience.

Closed-loop Feedback

With customer profiles updated in real time, the business can measure the effectiveness of campaigns and support interventions with greater precision. This closed-loop feedback loop makes it easier to optimize the customer journey and measure ROI across every touchpoint.

Key Deliverables & System Scope

A deep dive into the custom technical modules engineered to bring the system to life.

🛒

Headless Storefront

Designed and built a custom, lightning-fast storefront optimized for high-ticket electronics assets. Implemented flexible B2B wholesale portals alongside standard consumer checkouts.

💳

Stripe Payments

Integrated multi-currency Stripe capabilities complete with localized European tax calculations (VAT), automated digital invoices, and secure payment processing.

🔗

HubSpot & CRM Sync

Engineered n8n automated flows to stream client activities, post-purchase records, and support tickets instantly between HubSpot and the warehousing stack.
