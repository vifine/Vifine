Product Information Management System

Single source of truth for 10,000+ SKUs — bilingual content, multi-supplier pricing, and AI-generated imagery for a contract-manufactured furniture catalog

Role

Architect

Company

FullFlat LLC

Location

UAE

Year

2025

Core Tech

Airtable Shopify API n8n Airpower AI Image Generation

The Challenge

The client had no structured way to manage site content — product data lived disconnected from a Google Drive full of unlinked images, with no system tying visuals to SKUs. The storefront ran in two languages (Arabic and English), with no workflow to manage translations. Because products were sourced from multiple contract manufacturers, Shopify's single-cost-per-product model couldn't support real unit economics — the same product could carry a different cost depending on which manufacturer fulfilled it, and Shopify had no way to represent that.

Overview

Single source of truth for all product data across the business, for a furniture brand sourcing from multiple contract manufacturers and selling through a bilingual (English/Arabic) storefront. Manages the full product lifecycle — from initial creation to Shopify publication. The system serves three key roles: content manager (product data, translations, and visuals), procurement (multi-supplier pricing), and SEO specialist. AI-generated interior imagery replaces costly 3D rendering for lifestyle content.

Solution

The system manages the full product lifecycle across two entry paths, a multi-supplier cost structure, bilingual content, and multi-channel publishing to Shopify and beyond.

Product Entry & Supplier Feeds

Products enter PIM through two paths. Own furniture is created directly in the system — product, variants, materials, and 3D-rendered visuals. Each variant's SKU is generated through an automated, AI-assisted process built on a Material/Color/Size template, with a built-in uniqueness check that catches collisions before a SKU is saved — keeping codes consistent across thousands of variant combinations with zero manual entry. Accessories are imported from supplier feeds with automated sync of catalog data, pricing, stock levels, and images. The feed interface tracks sync status and update logs for every product, ensuring data consistency between supplier systems and PIM.

Multi-Supplier Cost Structure

Since products are sourced from different contract manufacturers at different prices, cost couldn't live on the product record alone. A dedicated linked Product × Supplier pricing table ties cost to the specific product-supplier pair, giving accurate unit economics regardless of which manufacturer fulfills a given order — something Shopify's native single-cost-field model can't represent.

Bilingual Content & AI-Generated Imagery

Every product carries full English and Arabic content side by side, with Arabic translations managed directly by the content manager in Airtable. For lifestyle imagery, rendering each product into realistic interior scenes was costly with traditional 3D rendering — instead, product renders are run through a third-party AI interior-generation service against a fixed set of template scenes, producing lifestyle imagery at a fraction of the cost and time.

Enrichment, SEO & Publishing

Each product goes through a structured enrichment process: taxonomy mapping, product attributes and metafields, SEO titles and descriptions. The SEO specialist works in a dedicated view across 1,000+ products, tracking readiness status for each item.

Ready products are published to Shopify with full control over publishing channels — Online Store, Meta, Google & YouTube, Pinterest. Each product carries its taxonomy-driven collections, tags, metafields, and media. Sync status is tracked per product with two methods: Airpower for bulk updates and Shopify API for individual products with variants and bundles.
