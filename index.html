Hey, I'm Victoria — 15+ years in e-commerce and business automation. Based in Israel, I work in English and Russian.

I started in digital marketing and worked my way up to running sales, marketing, supply, fulfillment, warehouse, and finance — the whole operational side of a business, not just one piece of it.

That's usually what gets missed when someone hires a Shopify developer. The store launches fine, but three months later the inventory doesn't match across channels, orders are still tracked in a spreadsheet, and nobody fully trusts the numbers. Nobody thought to plan for that at the start.

I build for that from the beginning — the systems and automation that keep the business running once the store is live, not just the store itself.

Happy to help with anything that speeds up your e-commerce.
